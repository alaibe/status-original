# Disclaimer

Status Original is software, not a service. There is no company behind it
holding anything on your behalf, and that is the point of it. It also means the
responsibilities below are yours, because there is nobody else they could fall
to.

## Your funds are yours alone

Your keys are generated on your device from your recovery phrase and are never
transmitted. The developer never has them, never has your funds, and never has
any way to act on your account.

Nobody can recover a lost recovery phrase. Nobody can reverse, cancel or freeze
a transaction you signed, recover funds sent to a wrong address, or restore an
account whose phrase is gone. Write the words down and keep them offline.

The app is not an exchange, a broker or a custodian. It sells nothing, holds
nothing, and takes no fee on any transaction. Nothing in it is financial
advice. Token prices, swap quotes and balances are read from public sources and
shown as they are found; they can be wrong, stale, or manipulated by whoever
controls the source. Check what a transaction says before you confirm it,
because once it is signed and broadcast it cannot be taken back.

## Your conversations are yours alone

The developer runs no server that your messages pass through. They travel over
whichever network the conversation is on, to relays, homeservers and accounts
you chose. Your messages are never seen, stored, transmitted or moderated by
the developer, who therefore cannot read a conversation, retrieve a message,
remove one, or intervene in one.

You are responsible for what you send and for what you do with what you
receive, including complying with the law where you are. Anyone you talk to can
keep a copy of what you send them, as with any messenger. End-to-end encryption
protects a message in transit; it does not control what the person at the other
end does with it afterwards.

## What other people run is theirs

Relays, Matrix homeservers, bridges, Telegram, blockchain endpoints, market
data providers, LI.FI, bots and any site you connect a wallet to are operated
by other people. Choosing to use one is your decision, and how it behaves is
between you and whoever runs it. The app tells you, on each conversation and
before each connection, what that choice exposes.

A bot is an address that answers, and nobody vets bots. One sees everything you
send it, exactly as a person would.
